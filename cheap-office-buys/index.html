10. A small rug under the desk

Also not a product link, because almost anything works.

In a room that is also a bedroom or a living room, a rug under the desk and chair defines the workspace as a separate zone. It sounds like decorating advice and it is genuinely functional — the boundary is most of what makes the corner feel like somewhere you work rather than a desk that happens to be in your bedroom.

Three that are not worth it

Cable sleeves and braided wraps. They look tidy in product photos and bundle everything into one thick tube — tidy until you need to change a single cable, then you unwrap the whole thing. Trays and clips let you pull one cable without touching the rest.

Multi-tier desktop shelves. They occupy the back band of the desk and the vertical space above it simultaneously, and become dust-collecting surfaces. On a small desk they take more than they give.

"Desk organiser sets." Sets are sized for the photograph, not for your desk. Buy the individual piece that solves the problem you actually have.

If you are starting today

Cable tray, then riser, then clamp lamp. Live with those three for two weeks, see what is still ending up on the desk, and buy storage for that. Buying storage before you know what you are storing is the most common way to spend money and gain nothing.
